BowmasterPrelude

Hold the keep. One bow, endless waves.

Play it like an app: tap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. It runs full screen and works offline.

How to play

  • DrawTouch anywhere on the field and drag backwards, like pulling a bowstring. The further you pull, the harder the shot.
  • LooseLift your finger. Arrows arc — lead your target and aim high for distance.
  • Aim for the headHeadshots do far more damage, pay 50% extra gold and drop most footmen in one hit.
  • ShieldbearersTheir shield stops body shots. Go over the top, take the legs, or punch through with a Bodkin.
  • Special shaftsBuy fire, frost, bomb, bodkin and storm arrows in the shop. Tap one in the bottom bar to load it for your next shot.
  • Between wavesSpend gold on your bow, your keep and militia to hold the line. Upgrades last for the whole run.
  • The keepAnything that reaches the wall chips away at it. At zero, the run ends.
