## World reference interpretation
The supplied map shows Greenwood upper-left, mountains upper-right, Iron Hills mid-right, Sunscorch mid-left, Frostmarch lower-right and Warlord realm lower-center. Retain these relative compositions for the strategic map. Journey progression still follows the numbered kingdoms; a road can curve west/east between regions. “Journey south” is campaign direction, not a demand every segment has negative north coordinate. Cold biomes can be high-elevation.

Keep capital on hill, river network, waterfalls, giant bridges, foothills, warm desert city, purple winter citadel and looming dark-red final stronghold as signature landmarks. The image is a world concept, not a measured heightmap/navmesh. Author exact terrain and roads through a coordinate/route specification after repository scale audit. Ocean/cloud borders in concept can become natural boundaries and fog in game.

The camera strip confirms four presentation scales: Kingdom elevated, Adventure rear-trailing, Combat close behind, Cinematic overlook. Cinematic is a temporary overlay, not a fourth control scheme. Do not copy the noisy small resource-node legend as finished UI; use resource asset IDs and clean icon design.

The six new bosses and troop names are working proposals created for this workup, not previously agreed canon. Existing army role mechanics survive. Regional variants add a bounded thematic tactic; do not duplicate entire game systems per faction.
