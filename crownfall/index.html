Ride out. Gather gold. Build a kingdom that holds.

Play it like an app: tap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.

How to play

  • 🐎 RideTouch anywhere and drag to steer the king. He shoots the nearest enemy all by himself.
  • 🎥 LookDrag with two fingers to look around, pinch to zoom. Tap 👑 to snap back to the king.
  • 🪙 GoldFallen enemies drop coins. Ride over them and they stack up on your horse. Take the Mountain Fort in the east to dig for more.
  • 🏗️ PadsA pad turns green when you can afford it. Stand on it to build.
  • 👑 The castleTap the castle to upgrade the king (bow, horse, weapons) and the castle and walls. You need the full cost to buy anything.
  • ⚔️ The armyBuild Barracks and tap them to train knights, archers and raiders.
  • 🔨 PlacingBetween waves tap Build, pick a building, then drag it on the grid and tap Build here.
  • 🪓 GatheringStand still by forest trees and the king chops wood; by highland boulders he mines stone. Carry it back to the castle or an outpost to bank it.
  • ❤️ HealingDuring a wave the king only heals at the castle — ride back inside the green square. If he falls during a wave, the wave is lost and you fight it again.
  • 🗼 DefencesTap a tower to upgrade it: first with gold, then wood, then stone. Gates upgrade the same way from the castle.
  • 🔨 BuildBetween waves, tap Build to put houses, farms and workshops anywhere inside your walls.
  • 👷 WorkersTap a mine, lumber camp, quarry, farm or warehouse and use + to assign free villagers. More workers means faster resources, so build houses to grow your village.
  • 📦 WarehouseWoodcutters and masons stack what they cut at their camp. Warehouse haulers wheel it home, 5 at a time, and only then can you spend it. Ride past a warehouse to bank the coins on your horse: banked gold is safe and still spends.
  • ⛺ OutpostsTap an outpost to upgrade the king and train troops there. Each claims the land around it (marked with flags) for houses, farms and workshops. Two more towers and a catapult can go up beside it.
  • ☄️ CatapultsBuilt with stone, they lob boulders into the thickest pack of enemies. Upgrade one into a Trebuchet with iron.
  • ⚙️ IronPast the Riverford Outpost lie the Iron Hills. Build the Iron Mine, then a Blacksmith to forge iron tools and arrowheads.
  • 📊 RatesTap gold, wood or stone at the top to see how much you get per minute and from where.
  • 🚩 Follow meTap 🚩 and your soldiers ride with you; tap again to send them back to their posts. At a tower, call posted archers down one at a time.
  • 👥 PeopleHouses bring villagers. They work the mine, chop wood in the forest and cut stone, then haul it home. Soldiers are villagers who enlist.
  • ⚔️ WavesWhen you are ready, tap Start Wave. If the castle falls, you can retry the wave.
  • ⛺ The roadThe enemy road leads south to their stronghold. Clear enough waves and build outposts along it to claim new land, then lay siege: break the gate and bring down the keep to win the level.
