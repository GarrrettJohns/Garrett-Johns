STUDWARS

Knights vs Pirates

How to play

  • Select: tap a unit. Double-tap to grab every unit of that kind on screen. Long-press and drag (or tap Group) to box a crowd.
  • Move & fight: with units selected, tap the ground to march there. They fight anything they meet on the way. Tap an enemy to attack it.
  • Retreat: tap your own Keep with soldiers selected to pull them back.
  • Bricks: select builders and tap a tree or a brick pile. They carry bricks back to the Keep or a Depot.
  • Build: select a builder, pick a building, tap where it goes, then Build.
  • Farms let you have a bigger army. Barracks train soldiers. Towers shoot invaders.
  • Hero: your King is strong and comes back if he falls. Use his Rally power in a big fight.
  • Studs pop out of everything you smash. Walk over them to collect them.
  • Win by knocking down the Pirate Fort. Lose your Keep and it's over.
  • Camera: drag to look around, pinch to zoom, tap the map in the corner to jump.
